# Negotiation War Room — Assets

This skill does not require pre-loaded assets.

All outputs are generated dynamically based on the user's specific negotiation context:

- Intelligence Dossier (compiled from 6 parallel research agents)
- ZOPA & Anchor Map
- Concession Ladder
- Objection Handling Playbook (10 scenarios)
- Scenario Decision Trees (5 branching paths)
- Written Negotiation Templates (5 email/message templates)
- One-Page Battle Card (the hero deliverable)
- Post-Negotiation Scorecard (debrief mode)
